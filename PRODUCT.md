# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers using shadcn/ui, tweakcn, and Tailwind who want harmonious, ready-to-use themes without manually constructing a full token palette. They browse, compare, preview, and copy themes while building product interfaces.

## Product Purpose

mindfulcn turns Alex Cristache's mindful palettes into free, curated shadcn/ui themes. It helps developers quickly evaluate each palette in realistic interfaces and copy tweakcn-ready CSS with no account or setup.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, adaptive. The product should feel technically credible and quietly considered, with the selected theme becoming part of the identity rather than decoration around it.

## Anti-references

Avoid literal wellness imagery such as lotus flowers, meditating figures, spa motifs, and soft lifestyle styling. The identity may suggest balance or mindfulness through proportion, rhythm, and negative space without illustrating those concepts.

## Design Principles

- Let the selected theme shape the product's identity.
- Keep the interface subordinate to browsing and evaluating themes.
- Express mindfulness through balance and restraint, not wellness clichés.
- Use familiar developer-tool patterns with precise, accessible feedback.
- Preserve a coherent identity across light, dark, browser, and installed-app contexts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Theme adaptation must maintain clear contrast in light and dark modes. Brand state changes should not depend on motion, and identity must remain recognizable without color. Interactive surfaces should meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations.
